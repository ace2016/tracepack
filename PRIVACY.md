# Privacy in Tracepack

_Last updated 9 August 2026_

This document mirrors the privacy policy published at
[docs.tracepack.org](https://docs.tracepack.org). If the two ever disagree, the version on
`docs.tracepack.org` is the current one; open an issue so this copy can be brought back in
sync.

Tracepack is a product of SPENDMITA LTD. The current workspace is local first. Evidence added
to a pack is stored in your browser on your device and is not automatically uploaded to us.

**The short version.** No Tracepack account is required. We cannot see evidence held only in
your local workspace. You decide when to export or send it elsewhere.

## 2. Evidence in your local workspace

Project details, evidence files, notes, privacy decisions, and exports are processed in your
browser and stored using browser storage. They are not sent to SPENDMITA LTD merely because you
use Tracepack. We do not have access to local evidence and cannot recover it for you.

If you export a pack, download a project file, use another product's Send to Tracepack button,
or share evidence with another person, you control that action and destination. The sending
product and recipient may have their own privacy responsibilities.

## 3. Information we may receive

- **Website and security data.** Our hosting infrastructure may process IP address, request
  time, requested page, browser information, and security events to deliver and protect the
  sites.
- **Messages you send us.** If you contact us, we receive your contact details and the contents
  of your message.
- **GitHub activity.** Issues, pull requests, and other public contributions are processed by
  GitHub and are visible according to your GitHub account and repository settings.

## 4. Why we use this information

- To deliver the websites and protect them from abuse, relying on our legitimate interests in
  operating a secure service.
- To answer support, privacy, and contribution enquiries, relying on steps requested by you and
  our legitimate interests in supporting the product.
- To meet legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary.

## 5. Cookies, analytics, and local preferences

The current Tracepack sites do not use advertising cookies or application analytics. The
workspace uses local browser storage to keep projects and remember whether the introduction has
been completed. This local preference is not sent to us.

## 6. Service providers and transfers

The Tracepack sites are hosted on infrastructure provided by Hetzner. GitHub provides the
public source repository and contribution service. These providers process limited data under
their own terms and our applicable arrangements. Where personal data is transferred
internationally, we use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism.

## 7. How long we keep information

Local workspace data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the relevant browser
data, or replace your browser profile. We keep server security logs and support communications
only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, support, legal, and accountability
purposes. GitHub retains public contribution records according to its service and repository
history.

## 8. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for having a lawful reason to collect, use, and share personal data placed
in a pack. Review private information before export, share only what is necessary, and protect
exported files. Tracepack privacy prompts support your review but do not decide whether
disclosure is lawful.

## 9. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability, and objection. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw
it. These rights apply to information SPENDMITA LTD actually holds. We cannot access or delete
evidence that exists only in your browser.

Contact hello@spendmita.com to make a request. You may also complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## 10. Children

Tracepack is not directed at children. Do not use it to collect or share a child's personal
data unless you have an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards.

## 11. Tracepack Cloud

Tracepack Cloud is marked Coming Soon and is not part of the current local workspace. Before
any Cloud service begins processing personal data, we will explain what is collected, why it
is needed, where it is stored, who receives it, and how long it is kept. We will update this
notice before launch.
