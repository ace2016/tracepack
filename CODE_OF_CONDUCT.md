# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Tracepack is a small, focused project. The standard is simple: be respectful, assume good
faith, and disagree about technical decisions without making it personal.

Examples of behaviour that helps this project:

- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on the code, the design, or the decision, never the person who made it.
- Being patient with people who are new to the codebase or new to open source.
- Owning mistakes, and correcting course without defensiveness.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments, in issues, pull requests, commits, or any
  other project space.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing someone else's private information (physical or email address, etc.) without
  explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or bad-faith arguing after a decision has been made and
  explained.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention, in any project space.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, code review)
and to anyone representing the project in public spaces (an official social media account, or
acting as an appointed representative at an event).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard, and may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate it, or
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
